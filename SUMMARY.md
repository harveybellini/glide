# Agents for Humans Hackathon

**Sponsor:** AWS / Amazon (Managed by Devpost)  
**Total Prize Pool:** $40,000 in cash  
**Deadline:** Sep 15, 2026 @ 1:00 AM GMT+1 (6 days remaining)  
**Eligibility:** Above legal age of majority in country of residence (specific countries/territories excluded). Open to developers worldwide (solo or team).  
**Format:** Online, Public | Beginner Friendly, Machine Learning / AI, Open Ended  

---

## 💡 Overview & Goal
Every day, people lose hours to repetitive tasks like paying bills, scheduling meetings, or filling out paperwork. AWS challenges participants to build an autonomous AI agent using the **Strands Agents SDK** that handles routine tasks quietly in the background and only surfaces when a real decision needs to be made.

### Why Join?
* **$40,000 Cash Prizes** across three main tracks.
* **$50 AWS Credits** available to cover build costs (request via the Resources tab).
* **Bonus Points:** Earn bonus points by publishing your build journey on [builder.aws.com](https://builder.aws.com) using "Agents for Humans" in the title.

---

## 🎯 Hackathon Tracks

1. **Everyday Agents**
   * *Focus:* Automate busywork in daily life, home, money, health, errands, or family.
   * *Ideal:* Runs quietly in the background and pings only for key decisions.

2. **Professional Agents**
   * *Focus:* Target repetitive, judgment-heavy tasks that eat up the workday for professionals, makers, creators, or small-business owners.

3. **Good Neighbor Agents**
   * *Focus:* Solutions designed for groups, neighborhoods, nonprofits, food banks, schools, libraries, or local community organizations.

## 📋 Submission Requirements

* **Text Description:** Explain what the project does, target audience, and workflow.
* **Public Code Repository:**
  * Complete source code, assets, and setup instructions.
  * Licensed under **MIT** or **Apache** open-source license (visible in About section).
  * `README.md` and Architecture Diagram.
* **Demo Video (Max 5 minutes):**
  * Live demonstration of the working project.
  * Pitch covering: (1) Problem solved, (2) Target audience, (3) Importance.
  * *Format:* Slides, screen recordings, voiceovers allowed (no facecam required).
* **AWS Builder ID**
* **(Optional) Live Demo Link:** Projects with a live demo score higher on Technical Implementation. Deploying with **AgentCore** also strengthens this score.

## ⚖️ Judging Criteria

* **Technological Implementation:** Skillful and non-trivial use of Strands Agents SDK. Bonus for live demo and/or Amazon Bedrock AgentCore deployment.
* **Design:** Complete, coherent product experience (beyond a simple PoC).
* **Potential Impact:** Credible case and effective solution for a real-world problem.
* **Creativity & Originality:** Non-obvious use of Strands Agents and strong domain understanding.
* **Presentation:** Clear end-to-end video demonstration and pitch.
