## 👤 Eligibility Guidelines

### Who CAN Participate
* **Individuals:** Must be of the legal age of majority in their country of residence [cite: 3].
* **Teams:** Groups of eligible individuals (must appoint one authorized Representative) [cite: 3].
* **Organizations:** Corporations, non-profits, LLCs, and partnerships [cite: 3].

### Ineligible Jurisdictions & Restrictions
* **Excluded Locations:** Residents/Organizations from Australia, Argentina, Brazil, Hong Kong, Indonesia, Italy, Malaysia, Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam, Singapore, Belarus, DNR, LNR, UAE, Quebec (Canada), Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, and OFAC-sanctioned regions [cite: 3].
* **Excluded Parties:** Employees, agents, judges, or immediate family/household members of AWS or Devpost [cite: 3].

## 📋 Project & Submission Requirements

### Technical & Code Requirements
* **SDK:** Built using the **Strands Agents SDK** [cite: 4].
* **New Work Only:** Projects must be newly created during the submission window. Pre-existing frameworks/tools must be disclosed [cite: 4].
* **Repository:** Public repository (GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket) with an **MIT or Apache** open-source license file detectable at the top of the repo [cite: 4].
* **Documentation:** Must include a detailed `README.md` and an **Architecture Diagram** [cite: 4].
* **Deployment (Optional):** Live demo link and/or deployment via **Amazon Bedrock AgentCore** increases Technical Implementation score [cite: 4].

### Video & Presentation Requirements
* **Length:** Maximum 5 minutes [cite: 4].
* **Hosting:** Uploaded publicly to YouTube or Vimeo [cite: 4].
* **Content:** Must show a working demo and a pitch covering:
  1. The problem being solved [cite: 4]
  2. The target audience [cite: 4]
  3. Why the solution matters [cite: 4]

## ⚖️ Evaluation & Scoring

### Stage One: Pass/Fail
* Preliminary screening to verify theme alignment and baseline technical usage of required SDKs/APIs [cite: 5].

### Stage Two: Scored Criteria (1-5 Points Base)
1. **Technical Implementation:** Depth and skill in using Strands Agents SDK [cite: 5].
2. **Design:** Complete and coherent user experience beyond a proof-of-concept [cite: 5].
3. **Potential Impact:** Credible real-world solution for a defined audience [cite: 5].
4. **Creativity & Originality:** Unique and non-obvious application of agent capabilities [cite: 5].
5. **Presentation:** Quality of demo, clarity of pitch, and ease of understanding [cite: 5].

### 🌟 Bonus Points (Up to +0.6)
* Publish public blog post(s) on **builder.aws.com** describing your build journey [cite: 4, 5].
* Must include **Agents for Humans** in the title [cite: 4, 5].
* Each post adds **+0.2 points** to Stage Two score (max +0.6 total) [cite: 5].
* Maximum final potential score: **5.6 points** [cite: 5].
